# schedulebuilderExportCUNY
exports classes, dates & times for CUNY's schedulebuilder. 

# instructions

## step 1

click the green button 'code' and download as zip

<img width="730" height="636" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae11945c-6ed0-4c45-8ad3-a9383e7e9a28" />


## step 2 

unzip the file, you should see a folder now

## step 3
Go to
chrome://extensions/ OR 
brave://extensions/ OR 
whatever browser you're using
<img width="248" height="37" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-08 at 2 00 54 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d0b99177-ae49-4b61-9aae-bd0e142cbc8f" />


## step 4

Turn on developer mode at the top right of the page. [if applicable]

<img width="220" height="72" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-08 at 2 01 11 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/560135a1-6625-4171-abb7-a39e95e571e4" />


## step 5

click 'load unpacked'
and select the file that you unzipped earlier.

## step 6

enable the extension, visit schedulebuilder 

## step 6

login, select the semester you want to export
look at the bottom right, click export, download and then open the file or upload it to calender.
