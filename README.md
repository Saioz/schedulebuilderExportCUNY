# schedulebuilderExportCUNY
exports classes, dates & times for CUNY's schedulebuilder. 

# How to install the extension? (Takes 2 minutes) 

## step 1

click the green button 'code' and download as zip

<img width="500" height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae11945c-6ed0-4c45-8ad3-a9383e7e9a28" />


## step 2 

unzip the file, you should see a folder now
<img width="521" height="49" alt="Screenshot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ed0e5f8a-95fe-45f4-b324-42f6ef601f36" />


## step 3
Go to <br />
chrome://extensions/ OR <br />
brave://extensions/ OR <br />
whatever browser you're using<br />
<img width="248" height="37" alt="Screenshot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d0b99177-ae49-4b61-9aae-bd0e142cbc8f" />


## step 4

Turn on developer mode at the top right of the page.

<img width="220" height="72" alt="Screenshot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/560135a1-6625-4171-abb7-a39e95e571e4" />


## step 5

click 'load unpacked'
and select the file that you unzipped earlier.
<img width="487" height="25" alt="Screenshot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c8e6da81-68ab-4aff-9e90-fcfcc11cab5e" />


## step 6

enable the extension, visit schedulebuilder 
<img width="800" height="500" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9a2e4eff-67bb-40fb-aa5e-2af9160f3db4" />


## step 6

login, select the semester you want to export
look at the bottom right, click export, download and then open the file or upload it to calender. 

<img width="205" height="71" alt="Screenshot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ccfd82f4-1113-4c0a-8d3c-3ff4cb94bbfc" />
